About Green Cycle

Green Cycle is a student-driven recycling platform for the Faculty of Pharmacy at Alexandria University. Students earn green points by participating in on-campus recycling, environmental events, and surveys. Points contribute to a leaderboard that recognises the most active recyclers each semester.

تطبيق Green Cycle مبادرة طلابية لإعادة التدوير داخل كلية الصيدلة جامعة الإسكندرية. يحصل الطلاب على نقاط خضراء عند المشاركة في أنشطة إعادة التدوير والفعاليات البيئية والاستبيانات داخل الحرم الجامعي، ويتنافسون على لوحة المتصدرين.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up?

Open the app, tap "Create Account", enter your name, phone number, and a password, and upload a clear photo of your Faculty of Pharmacy student ID card. Verification typically takes 1–2 business days.

My ID card was rejected. What now?

Email us at greencycleau@gmail.com with your phone number and a fresh photo of your ID. We'll review it manually.

How do I earn green points?

You can earn points by depositing recyclable materials in campus bins, joining environmental events, and completing surveys posted in the app.

How can I delete my account?

Please send an account deletion request to greencycleau@gmail.com from the email address (or phone number) associated with your account. We will permanently delete your profile and associated data within 7 business days and reply with confirmation.

Where can I find the privacy policy?

See the Privacy Policy page.

The app shows a loading spinner that never finishes

This usually means a temporary network issue. Please make sure you have a stable internet connection, fully close the app, and reopen it. If the problem persists for more than a few minutes, email us and we'll investigate.

Your Privacy

We collect only the information needed to verify your enrollment and run the recycling program — your name, phone number, and a photo of your student ID card. We do not share your personal data with third parties. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
